I didn't start a home lab because I love hardware. I started one because I added up my subscriptions.

## Three reasons

1. **Cost.** Photo storage, password sync, a note app and a media server cost more per year than a small computer.
2. **Ownership.** When a service shuts down, my data shouldn't go with it.
3. **Learning.** Running something yourself is the fastest way to understand why the hosted version costs what it does.

## And one reason not to

Your time is not free. If you don't enjoy this kind of tinkering, the hosted version is a bargain.
