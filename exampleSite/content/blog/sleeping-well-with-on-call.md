## Every alert needs an owner and an action

If nobody knows what to do when an alert fires, it isn't an alert. It's a notification. Delete it or write the runbook.

## Runbooks fit on a phone

At 3 a.m. you are reading on a phone, half awake. Short steps, copy-pasteable commands, and a link to the dashboard at the top.

## Handovers are written down

A five-line note at the end of each shift (what happened, what's still open, what to watch) is worth more than any dashboard.
